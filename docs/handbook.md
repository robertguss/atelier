# Atelier handbook

How we work in the Atelier room. Every participant reads this, human and model.
The weekly retro may propose changes; Robert approves them.

1. **Fight scope creep.** Don't build something until the thing it replaces
   has hurt at least twice in real use.
2. **Critics bring receipts.** You can only block work with a failing test, a
   repro command or a concrete trace.
3. **Disagree on purpose.** A review ends with a real objection, or with "I
   tried to break this, and here's what I tried."
4. **The ledger is append-only.** Nothing that matters happens without being
   recorded.
